Updates:
- The map has been rebuilt from the ground up for Warcraft III 2.0 (new script engine)
- Race selection is a panel: browse races by tier, read what each one does, then pick. The race shops are gone
- Hybrid random gets a build menu with your nine rolled towers: B opens it, Q W E R A S D F Z X C pick a tower, Escape closes. Click the grid to build; hold shift to keep building and to queue towers behind the builder, shown as ghosts
- The host picks game mode and difficulty, or hands it to a vote. Votes no longer block the screen; the difficulty vote runs while races are picked
- New action bar: sample maze, claim towers, build menu, race selection, range check
Updates:
- -gray: when the gray lane is empty, take it over. Your towers, builders and units move with you and you become the last defender
- When the gray player leaves, everyone is offered the lane. The first to take it moves there at once, or as soon as gray is clear of creeps
- Range check: -range or the action bar button draws the attack range of any tower you select as a sharp ring at any size
- Towers you build between waves sell for their full price until the next wave starts; once a wave is running, selling refunds 75% as before
- Difficulty scales creep health and armor only (a hidden second multiplier is gone)
- New loading screen
Updates:
- Fixed: void fragments are refunded when a bought building is cancelled
- Fixed: the last row of the Blue, Gray, Brown and Maroon lanes can be built on
- Fixed: abilities broken in the rewrite (Void items, base damage of several towers, Draenei Seer, Ancient Protector, Hydralisk, Adventurer, Cavern Hermit, Pay the Toll)
- Fixed: hybrid towers could be built on checkpoints
- Fixed: Wyvern lightning range, Iron Golem spike directions
- Fixed: Wild Shape handed 100 gold back
- Fixed: the custom UI flickered under the mouse
- Anti-block and anti-juggle are faster and cancel instantly
- Host detection works on every platform, so host-only settings are reliable
Updates:
- Buttons on the race, vote and settings panels react the moment you click, without waiting on the network
- A vote ends as soon as everyone has voted
- Creep abilities by difficulty: one per 100% above Normal (Medium 1, Hard 2, Extreme 3), and bosses get all ten
- Venom Tower attacks a random enemy in range
- Loot Boxer: higher tiers give fewer Rocks, tiers 7-9 none; every roll is logged (-log). Loot Boxer is random-only
- Repick starts over: the same race again, or Hybrid Random after any pick
- Shrine of Buffs is a secondary race, picked from its own tab once you have a race
- The host can set mode and difficulty with -s (for host bots)
Updates:
- Fixed: Elementalist runes could never roll Life
- Fixed: Loot Boxer tier 4-9 boxes always gave Rocks, and Rocks lost their charges
- Fixed: Wyvern lightning skipped Navy's creeps and spared waves 34-35 instead of the bosses
- Fixed: votekick could never pass; it now needs a majority
- Fixed: the scoreboard wrote the first player over its header
- Fixed: High Priest's Monsoon cancelled its attacks; Berserker and Flesh Golem never berserked
- Fixed: Pay the Toll failed silently without 50 gold; Void Restoration did nothing
- Fixed: Ice Troll Priest could Frost Nova dead creeps
- Fixed: Alliance of Blades enchantment checks and refunds
- Fixed: desyncs when a race was picked
Updates:
- Elementalist: grown towers gain Attunement every wave (+2% damage a level, up to 15) and keep it when they fuse
- Primals: Siphon fuses two grown towers into a Primal, for gold. The Lich (Undead L2 + Death Rune L3) keeps the Undead's damage and grows every wave; the Thunderhead (Air Rune L3 + Water Rune L3) strikes air with chaos splash
- Surge buys a Primal Attunement, 60 gold a level, 10 at most; at Attunement 15 it can Ascend into the Lich King or the Eye of the Storm
- Discover the recipes: the first Siphon on an untried pair of grown towers shows what it makes, for free, and the next makes it. A pair with no recipe says so. The recipe book (action bar, or -book) lists what you have found; it is saved on your computer and loads in your next game
- Thunderhead model: Pandarian Storm Spire by Remixer
